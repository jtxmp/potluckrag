# Onboarding notes

## First day

- Ask IT for a laptop and a hardware security key.
- Join #general, #releases and #incidents.
- Clone the monorepo and run `make setup`. It takes about 20 minutes.

## First week

- Pair with your buddy on one small bug fix and ship it.
- Read the architecture overview in the wiki.
- Shadow one on-call shift. You do not get paged in your first month.

## Who to ask

- Builds and CI: the platform team.
- Access and accounts: IT, through the help desk form.
- Anything else: your buddy, then your manager.
